# Research brief: AI analysis of gameplay video from user devices

Question from the product owner: beyond user research, what analyses can be done on gameplay video captured from a player's device (churn analysis, tutorial drop-off, competitor analysis, and anything else), and which of these would a gaming company pay for as a service?

Each research agent covers one angle and writes `<angle>.md` here with:
- Findings as short factual bullets, each with the source URL inline.
- A clear split between VERIFIED (stated by a source) and INFERRED (your reading).
- Named companies, products, papers, prices, buyers and numbers wherever a source gives them.
- A final section "Signals of willingness to pay": who buys, what they pay for, what they complain about.
No summary paragraphs. No recommendations. The ideation stage does that later.
Use WebSearch (mode "extended" for niche or recent facts) and WebFetch on the best sources. At least 8 searches per angle.
